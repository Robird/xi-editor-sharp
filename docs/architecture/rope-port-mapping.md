5. **Wire up Breaks/Diff/Search exporters** – CLI/schema/manifest 规范已落在 `[StageD::FixtureFlow]`、`[StageD::ParityAssets]`、`fixtures/parity-fixture-schema.md`；下一步是实现 `export-serde-fixtures` flag（`--breaks-descriptors`, `--diff-regions`, `--search-spans`）、写入 manifest，并在 `scripts/refresh_*` 中启用这些资产 (`[TS-B5]`).
